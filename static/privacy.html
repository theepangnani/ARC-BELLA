Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

ARC (“Ambient Response Core”) is a self-hosted, personal voice assistant. Each instance runs on the owner’s own computer. This policy explains what data ARC touches, why, and how it is protected — with particular attention to Google user data, which ARC handles in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

The short version: ARC runs on your own machine. Your data stays there. It is used only to carry out the requests you make, in the moment you make them. Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used to train any model.

Who this covers

ARC is operated privately by its owner for personal use. The “operator” is the individual running the ARC instance; “you” is anyone who signs in to that instance and connects an account.

Data ARC accesses

  • Google account data, only after you explicitly connect your account through Google’s standard OAuth consent screen:
    • Google Calendar (read & write) — to read your schedule and create or update events when you ask.
    • Gmail (read-only) — to read and summarise mail you ask about. ARC holds the gmail.readonly scope and nothing else: it cannot send, reply, draft, forward, delete, or change labels. Mail is only ever an input to ARC.
    • Google Contacts (read-only) — to look up who you mean when you name a person.
    • Google Drive (read-only) — to find and read files you ask about.
  • Voice and text you send — your spoken or typed requests, processed to produce a response.
  • Screen captures — only when you explicitly turn on the “Live screen” feature, and only to answer the question you asked about what’s on screen.
  • Local preferences — timers, watchlists, notes and settings you create, stored in your own browser.

How ARC uses your data

Your data is used for one purpose: to provide the assistant features you ask for — reading your calendar, summarising a message, answering a question about your screen, and similar tasks. That’s it.

What ARC does not do

  • It does not sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • It does not use your Google data for advertising.
  • It does not use your Google data to train, fine-tune, or improve any AI or machine-learning model.
  • It does not transfer your Google data to others except as needed to provide the feature you requested (see below), or where required by law.

Where your data goes

To understand a request and produce a reply, the relevant text — and, if you turned on Live screen, the screenshot — is sent to Anthropic’s Claude API, the AI model that powers ARC’s responses. Anthropic processes it to generate the answer and, per its API terms, does not use API inputs to train its models. Google data is retrieved directly from Google’s APIs to your instance. No other third parties receive your data.

Storage and retention

  • Your Google sign-in token is stored on the operator’s machine, scoped to your session, so ARC can act on your behalf until you disconnect.
  • Preferences and history you create live in your own browser (local storage), not on a shared server.
  • Requests are processed in the moment; ARC does not build long-term profiles of you. Delete your local data any time by clearing the site’s browser storage.

Revoking access

You can disconnect your Google account at any time using the Disconnect control inside ARC, or by removing ARC from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking immediately ends ARC’s access to your Google data.

Security

Connections are served over encrypted HTTPS. Access to an ARC instance requires signing in with Google, and is limited to an explicit list of addresses chosen by the operator — authenticating with Google is not by itself enough to get in. Google’s full consent screen is shown on every sign-in, and a session lasts at most four hours (and ends after thirty minutes idle) before you must sign in again. Sessions are held on the server and can be revoked at any time. Because ARC is self-hosted, your data is not pooled with other users on a central service.

Children

ARC is not directed to children under 13 and should not be used by them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change with it.
