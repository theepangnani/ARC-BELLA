Quick guide hide

1 · Talk to it
Hold the ring in the middle, speak, then let go. That always works, even with everything else switched off.

2 · Hands free
Tick Always listening. Then say the wake word — bella by default — and your question: "bella, what's the weather."

3 · Follow-ups are free
For about fifteen seconds after a reply you can just keep talking. No wake word needed. After that, say the name again.

4 · Read the ring
SAY "BELLA" — waiting for you.
LISTENING… — collecting what you say.
MIC RECONNECTING… — wait a beat before speaking.
SAY "BELLA" FIRST — it heard you but wasn't addressed.

5 · Typing works too
Use the box on the right. Same assistant, no microphone needed.

6 · It remembers you
Durable things — your name, how you like answers — are kept on this machine between sessions. MEMORY shows how many. Forget all wipes them.

7 · If it goes quiet
Check HEARING on the left. LIVE means the microphone is on. Anything else means Chrome is reconnecting — give it a moment.

8 · Several people in the room
Tick Room mode. Say the wake word and your question together — "bella, what's the weather" — every single time. No free follow-ups, and the name must come at the start. ARC also stops assuming the same person is speaking, and files nothing to memory. Holding the ring is still the surest way to be the one it hears.

Tap the ring while it's talking to cut it off. Wake word and voice are changeable above.
