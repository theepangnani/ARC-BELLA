What it asks permission for

  • Google Calendar — read your schedule and create or update events you ask for.
  • Gmail — read and summarise mail you ask about, and draft or send messages on request. It cannot delete mail.
  • Google Contacts (read-only) — recognise the people you mention.
  • Google Drive (read-only) — find and read files you ask about.

You grant each of these on Google’s own consent screen, and can revoke them any time from your Google Account or ARC’s Disconnect button. ARC never sells your data, never uses it for ads, and never uses it to train any AI model. See the Privacy Policy for details.
